Dubai Experience

25–29 November 2026 · Dubai · Private flights from Dallas

Organised by HOFF Exclusive

  • Return private flights from Dallas
  • 4 nights · Atlantis The Royal
  • Up to 16 guests · By invitation

Atlantis The Royal

The programme includes four nights in a private suite at Atlantis The Royal on Palm Jumeirah, with daily breakfast. A HOFF host accompanies the group, with private chauffeur service during the stay.

The invitation lists Royal Club Lounge access, breakfast at Gastronomy, Nobu by the Beach and private beaches, a 24-hour e-butler, Aquaventure World, The Lost World Aquarium and AWAKEN Fitness among the guest privileges.

Your suite

The suite description includes a king-size bed, a separate living area, a private balcony and a marble bathroom, with views towards the Arabian Sea, the Palm or the skyline. The supplied images illustrate the accommodation presented in the invitation.

Yachts, skyline and desert

Dubai International Boat Show

The Boat Show visit at Dubai Harbour includes curated yacht viewings and private appointments for the HOFF group. It forms the main programme on 26 November.

Helicopter and desert

A private helicopter flight over Dubai is followed by a private 4×4 desert experience, sunset champagne in the dunes and dinner beneath the stars.

Private yacht day

A charter along the Dubai coastline includes lunch on board and time for water sports, followed by the HOFF closing evening.
