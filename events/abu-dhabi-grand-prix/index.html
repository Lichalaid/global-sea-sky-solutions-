Abu Dhabi Grand Prix Experience

2–7 December 2026 · Abu Dhabi · Private flights from Dallas

Organised by HOFF Exclusive

  • Return private flights from Dallas
  • 5 nights · Yas Island
  • Up to 16 guests · By invitation

Stay on Yas Island

Five nights in a five-star suite on Yas Island, daily breakfast, private dinners and chauffeur service are included in the invitation. A dedicated HOFF host accompanies the group throughout the programme.

The trip combines activities on the water and above the city with the Grand Prix weekend. Practice, qualifying and race day each have their own place in the itinerary, with the final race viewed from a superyacht in Yas Marina.

The Grand Prix weekend

Practice and circuit tour

Friday combines the practice sessions with a guided behind-the-scenes circuit tour. The invitation includes a private dinner after the day’s programme.

Qualifying and hospitality

Saturday brings drinks and networking in private hospitality before qualifying, followed by a private dinner.

Race day from a superyacht

Sunday begins with a private helicopter flight over Abu Dhabi. The Grand Prix is watched aboard a superyacht in Yas Marina, followed by dinner and the closing party.

A day on the Abu Dhabi coastline

Before the race weekend, Thursday is devoted to a private yacht day along the Abu Dhabi coastline. The itinerary starts with breakfast and finishes with a private dinner, placing time on the water alongside the circuit programme.
