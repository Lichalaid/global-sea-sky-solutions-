Sculptural art

Formulart and Speedart translate a single-seater and a racing motorcycle into metal sculpture. The supplied B.R.M collection presents each as a unique piece made in France, with openwork construction and individually finished bases.

Formulart

A single-seater in polished copper and aluminium, with an openwork structure and a brushed, riveted aluminium base. The brochure presents Formulart as a unique sculptural piece.

  • Polished copper and aluminium
  • Brushed, riveted aluminium base
  • Unique sculptural piece

Speedart

A racing motorcycle reduced to an openwork frame and a mirror-polished copper shell. Chrome and lacquer details complement a lacquered base with copper inlay.

  • Copper, chrome and lacquer
  • Lacquered base with copper inlay
  • Unique sculptural piece

Nautical clocks and watch winders

The nautical collection brings the propeller and the ship’s wheel into table clocks. Watch winders provide a different function: keeping automatic watches in motion inside a metal case.

Propeller clocks

Three blades machined from solid aluminium surround a B.R.M dial above a hull-shaped base. The body combines hand-applied lacquer and polished metal, with signal-flag engraving at the foot. The brochure presents numbered pieces made to order.

The ship’s wheel

Eight hand-polished copper handles are set into a lacquered rim above a hull-shaped base. The owner’s or vessel’s emblem can be discussed for the piece, retaining the collection’s metal construction and hand-applied finishes.

Watch winders

Cases for automatic watches, made in aluminium with a hinged lid and porthole. The supplied collection includes a double winder with three speed settings and reversible rotation.

  • Solid aluminium case
  • Hinged lid and porthole
  • Three speeds and reversible rotation

Four propeller-clock finishes

Finishes and personalisation

Material, lacquer colour, dimensions and engraving can be discussed with B.R.M through Mercedes. The owner’s emblem or a vessel’s symbol can form part of the conversation. Describe the piece and its intended setting so the request concerns the object you actually want.

Objects for yacht interiors

The brochure presents pieces for a main salon, sky lounge, owner’s suite, console or bar. The sculptures, clocks and winders give you different ways to bring a motor-racing or nautical object into that setting.

Collection questions

Are these wristwatches?

The collection shown here consists of sculptures, nautical table clocks and watch winders. A watch winder is a case for an automatic wristwatch, rather than a wristwatch itself.

Can I enquire about an emblem or engraving?

Yes. Mention the relevant piece and the engraving, colour or emblem you have in mind. The available choices are discussed for that object.
