Electric propulsion in the water

SEABOB is an electric water scooter for surface and underwater use. In the F9 series, power is adjusted with a thumb-operated accelerator. Normal, Dive and Sport modes provide different responses; Dive mode uses a more sensitive setting.

Colours and accessories

The F9 range shows Carex Yellow, Signum Red, Star White, Titanium, Meteo Silver and Navel Orange finishes. Manufacturer accessories include a quick charger, transport bag, protective cover and holding rack. Mention the model, colour and accessories that interest you.
