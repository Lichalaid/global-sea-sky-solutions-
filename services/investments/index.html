Do I need a complete project presentation to make contact?

You can start with a short overview of the business, idea, service or product and the connection you are looking for. Detailed information can be discussed directly with Mercedes.

Yes. Describe your interests and any market or location you are considering. The enquiry can start from your investment interest rather than a named project.
