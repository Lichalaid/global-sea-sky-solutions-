Athlete participation for your event

Global Sea Sky Solutions receives enquiries about athlete participation in events through Mercedes Pilla’s ASM connection. The starting point is the event you are organising and the role you would like to discuss with an athlete.

You may have a particular name in mind or be exploring what could suit the occasion. Explain the audience, the location and the kind of participation you want to ask about. Names and availability are discussed directly for that event.

Describe the occasion

Event and audience

Describe the occasion, who will attend and why an athlete’s participation is relevant to it. A short overview is enough for the initial enquiry.

City and venue

Include the city and, if already chosen, the venue. You can make contact while the location is still being planned.

Date or period

Share a confirmed date or an approximate period, along with the timing you have in mind for the athlete’s involvement.

The participation you have in mind

Tell us the athlete you want to ask about and what you would like them to do. Participation details are discussed specifically for the request.

Your ASM connection

Mercedes is the point of contact for this enquiry at Global Sea Sky Solutions. Keep the first message focused on the event and the participation you want to explore; the conversation about names and availability takes place directly.

Questions about athlete participation

Can I ask about a specific athlete?

Yes. Include the name and the event context in your message. This starts an enquiry about participation and availability through the ASM connection.

Do I need a final date and venue?

You can start with a city and an approximate period. Explain which parts of the event are already defined and which are still being planned.

Does the form confirm an athlete booking?

It sends you to an enquiry with Mercedes. The website does not confirm an athlete’s availability or issue a booking.
