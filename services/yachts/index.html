Yachts and motorboats

New, pre-owned and off-market

The search can start with an exact model, a brand you follow or a description of the yacht you want to own. The boats shown here are a starting point for the conversation; enquiries can concern other yachts worldwide.

New yachts

For a new yacht, describe the model or type of vessel, the layout you are considering and where you plan to use it. Moon Yacht is presented above with Sail imagery and collection interiors.

Pre-owned yachts

Share a yacht listing you have already seen or describe the size, accommodation and location you have in mind. The enquiry can focus on a particular vessel or a wider pre-owned search.

Off-market sourcing

Private offers may sit outside public listings. Mercedes also receives off-market search requests through her international connections. Share the type of vessel and location relevant to your search.

Support alongside your purchase

Yacht acquisition can involve more than choosing the vessel. The following services are part of the purchase support confirmed for Global Sea Sky Solutions enquiries. Discuss which ones are relevant to the yacht and destination you have in mind.

Financing

Discuss a financing enquiry for the yacht you intend to acquire. The request is considered in relation to the specific purchase.

Insurance

Raise insurance requirements alongside the purchase, including the vessel and the location where you plan to keep it.

Inspections

Ask about inspection support for a yacht under consideration, whether the search starts with a public listing or a private offer.

Registration

Discuss registration support in connection with the selected yacht and its intended destination.

Delivery

Include where you want to receive the yacht so delivery requirements can be discussed with your sales enquiry.

Translation

Translation support is available for communications associated with the purchase. Mention the language support you need.

Questions about yachts

Can you search for a yacht outside the brands shown?

Yes. The worldwide yacht search is open to the yacht or brand you request, including vessels not displayed on this site.

Can I enquire about a yacht charter?

Yes. Share your destination and travel plans with Mercedes for a charter enquiry through HOFF Exclusive.

Can I ask specifically about Moon 70 Sail?

Yes. Use the Moon 70 Sail information button. The enquiry retains the model name. The collection gallery includes interiors and deck spaces whose layout and options are confirmed for the selected version.

What information helps with a yacht search?

A brand or model, approximate size, intended use and preferred location are useful starting points. You can also share a vessel you have already found.
