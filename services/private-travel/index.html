Private flights, stays and experiences

HOFF Exclusive brings together air and water charters, luxury vacations and business events. Global Sea Sky Solutions receives your enquiry through Mercedes, whether you are interested in a published invitation programme or a destination of your own.

The published Dubai and Abu Dhabi trips combine return private flights from Dallas with accommodation, yacht experiences, event access, private dinners, transfers and a dedicated HOFF host. Each programme has its own dates and inclusions; open the event page to review the full trip.

Planning around your trip

Travel party and dates

Tell us where you are travelling from, the number of guests and any dates you have in mind. For the two published invitation trips, Dallas is the stated departure point.

The experience you want

Mention the event or destination and the parts that matter most to you, such as a yacht day, private aviation, accommodation or a hosted programme.

Travel questions

Do all experiences include the same services?

Each published programme lists its own flights, stay, activities and dates. A destination enquiry outside those programmes is discussed separately.

Can I ask about just a yacht or private flight?

Yes. Yacht charter and private aviation enquiries are also available as separate interests. Describe the destination, route and dates in your message.

Does submitting an enquiry reserve a place?

The form starts an enquiry with Mercedes. Attendance and trip participation are discussed directly; the website does not issue a booking confirmation.
