Aircraft purchase, sale and leasing

Start with the aviation service you need. An acquisition, an aircraft offered for sale and a leasing enquiry call for different information; each option below keeps that context when you contact us.

Buy an aircraft

An acquisition enquiry starts with how you intend to use the aircraft: the routes you fly, passenger needs and the kind of ownership you are considering. Mercedes connects your request with Smarter Aircraft.

  • Routes and typical travel distance
  • Passenger and cabin preferences
  • A make or model, if already selected

Sell an aircraft

If you own an aircraft you want to offer for sale, contact Mercedes with the make, model and a general description. Her Smarter Aircraft connection is the point of contact for the sales enquiry.

  • Aircraft make and model
  • Current location
  • A short description of the aircraft

Aircraft leasing

Enquire about a leasing arrangement in relation to the aircraft and use you have in mind. Mercedes can discuss the request through Smarter Aircraft.

  • Aircraft or category of interest
  • Intended use and location
  • The leasing arrangement you want to discuss

Private charter flights

A charter enquiry starts with a journey: where you are departing from, where you need to arrive, travel dates and the number of passengers. Include a return flight or another stop if it forms part of your plans.

Private aviation is also part of the HOFF Exclusive travel and event programmes. The Dubai and Abu Dhabi experiences shown on this site include return private flights from Dallas.

Questions about private aviation

Can I enquire about one flight?

Yes. Use the private charter enquiry and include the route and travel dates you are considering.

Who is the aviation company?

Mercedes works as a broker in connection with Smarter Aircraft. HOFF Exclusive is also a connection for aircraft charters and luxury travel programmes.

Can I ask about an aircraft I already own?

Yes. Choose the aircraft sale enquiry and share the make, model, location and a brief description. You can raise a leasing request through the separate leasing option.

Is the Dubai departure point the same for all charters?

Dallas is the departure point stated for the two published HOFF invitation trips. For a separate charter, include your own departure point and destination.
